Every Skeuo4Ever feature in one package:
- Control Center
- Screenshot Preview
- Space Bar Cursor
- Swipe Back
- Low Power Mode and Battery Health
- Night Shift
- The "New" sash on every new app
- Bold Text
- Request Desktop Site

You can remove any feature separately later.

## Moving from iOS6Revive
The project used to be called iOS6Revive. This package replaces all the old iOS6Revive packages: Cydia removes them and installs the new ones. Settings and Activator gestures carry over by themselves.
