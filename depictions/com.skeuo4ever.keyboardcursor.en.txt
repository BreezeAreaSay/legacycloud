As in iOS 12: slide your finger along the space bar and the cursor moves through the text. Left and right move by letters, up and down by lines.

Sliding from the Shift key selects text: from where the cursor was to where your finger stops.

A normal tap on the space bar or Shift works as before: cursor control starts only once your finger moves. Works in every app, including text fields on websites in Safari.

## Settings
Settings → Skeuo4Ever → Space Bar Cursor.
