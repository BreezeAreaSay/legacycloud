Night Shift from iOS 9.3: after dark, the display colors shift to the warmer end of the spectrum.

- a "From… To…" schedule or "Manually Enable Until Tomorrow";
- a color temperature slider;
- smooth fading in and out.

Only the display colors change; the apps' interface stays the same. If the gamma table format turns out to be unfamiliar, the feature turns itself off and says so in its settings. A restart always brings the usual colors back.

## Settings
Brightness & Wallpaper → Night Shift, as in modern iOS. The same item is in Settings → Skeuo4Ever.
