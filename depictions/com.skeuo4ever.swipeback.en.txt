The gesture from iOS 7: swipe right from the left edge of the screen to go back to the previous screen.

The animation is the iOS 6 one: both screens slide together, with a light shadow along the edge. Let go before the middle and the screen slides back.

## When the gesture does not work
- on an app's first screen;
- when a custom button such as Cancel replaces the Back button — as in iOS 7;
- right after the screen rotates: the gesture works again after the next transition.

## Settings
Settings → Skeuo4Ever → Swipe Back.
